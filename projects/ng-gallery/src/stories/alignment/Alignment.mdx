## Snap Alignment & Index Accuracy

The gallery determines the `activeIndex` based on a **Snap Anchor**. The `snapAlign` property defines where that anchor sits in the viewport.

### Properties

| Input       | Type                           | Default    | Description                                                              |
|:------------|:-------------------------------|:-----------|:-------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| `snapAlign` | `'start' \| 'center' \| 'end'` | `'center'` | Sets the target "Anchor" point in the viewport.                          |
| `forceSnap` | `boolean`                      | `false`    | When true, adds dynamic padding to ensure every item can hit the Anchor. |

---

### How it Works: The "Anchor" Concept

To understand why `snapAlign` is necessary, imagine a physical "Target" or "Anchor" positioned in your gallery viewport:

* **`start`**: The Anchor is the **left edge** (or right in RTL).
* **`center`**: The Anchor is the **exact middle**.
* **`end`**: The Anchor is the **right edge** (or left in RTL).

An item only becomes "active" (`activeIndex`) when it physically reaches that Anchor.

#### The "Edge" Problem
By default, a scroll container stops as soon as the last item touches the end of the viewport.

* **In `start` mode**, the last item's *start edge* may never reach the *left edge* of the screen because there is no more content to scroll.
* **Result:** The last item can never be "active," and your `activeIndex` will stay stuck on a previous item.



---

### The `forceSnap` Solution

When you enable `forceSnap`, the gallery calculates the exact amount of "ghost space" (padding) needed at the boundaries.

1.  **Start Alignment + `forceSnap`**: Adds padding to the **end** of the gallery. This allows the last item to travel all the way to the start anchor.
2.  **End Alignment + `forceSnap`**: Adds padding to the **start** of the gallery. This allows the first item to travel all the way to the end anchor.
3.  **Center Alignment + `forceSnap`**: Adds padding to **both** sides. This ensures every item—from first to last—can be perfectly centered.

#### When should I use `forceSnap`?
* **Use it** if you need an accurate `activeIndex` for every item (e.g., for paginators or thumbnails).
* **Disable it** if you want a "standard" list feel where the scroll simply stops at the natural boundaries of the content.
